Hardware components

Identify the internal and external parts of a computer.

1. Internal components

  • Motherboard: the main circuit board that connects everything.
  • CPU (processor): carries out instructions; the “brain”.
  • RAM: fast temporary memory for running programs; it clears when power goes off.
  • Storage: HDD (spinning disk) or SSD (flash memory) holds the OS and files permanently.
  • Power supply unit (PSU): converts AC mains to the low-voltage DC the parts use.
  • Graphics card (GPU), cooling fans and heatsinks, and optional network or sound cards.

2. External devices and peripherals

  • Input: keyboard, mouse, scanner, microphone, webcam.
  • Output: monitor, printer, speakers.
  • External storage: flash drive, external hard drive, memory card.

Peripherals connect through ports or wirelessly (Bluetooth, Wi-Fi).

3. Ports and connectors

  • USB (Type-A and Type-C): keyboards, drives, charging.
  • HDMI, DisplayPort, VGA: video to monitors and projectors; VGA is older and analogue.
  • Ethernet (RJ-45): wired network. 3.5 mm jacks: audio.
  • Inside the case: SATA (HDD/SSD data), M.2 (small SSDs), PCIe (graphics and expansion cards), the 24-pin ATX power connector and the 4- or 8-pin CPU power connector.
