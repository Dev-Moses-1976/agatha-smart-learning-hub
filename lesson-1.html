Basics of computer hardware maintenance

What hardware maintenance is and why it matters.

1. What hardware maintenance means

Hardware is the physical part of a computer: anything you can touch. Maintenance is the work that keeps it running reliably. There are two kinds: preventive (scheduled cleaning, inspections and updates done before anything fails) and corrective (repairs done after a fault). Good maintenance extends the life of a computer, reduces breakdowns and protects the user’s data.

2. Safety and anti-static precautions

Static electricity can destroy components, often without you feeling it. Always:

  • Switch off, unplug the power cable and (for laptops) remove the battery where possible.
  • Wear an anti-static wrist strap clipped to bare metal on the case, or touch the metal case often.
  • Work on a clean, hard, dry surface, never on carpet.
  • Hold boards and cards by their edges and keep parts in anti-static bags.
  • Never open a power supply unit: it can hold a dangerous charge even when unplugged.

3. Basic tools of the trade

  • Screwdrivers: Phillips (the most common), flathead and small Torx.
  • Anti-static wrist strap, tweezers and a small parts tray for screws.
  • Compressed air or a blower, a soft brush, microfibre cloth and isopropyl alcohol (90% or higher).
  • Thermal paste, cable ties and a flashlight.
  • Multimeter and power supply tester.
  • A bootable USB flash drive with an operating system installer and diagnostic tools.
