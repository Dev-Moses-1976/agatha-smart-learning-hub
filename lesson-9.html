Routine maintenance

Corrective and preventive maintenance procedures.

1. Cleaning and dust control

  • Power off and unplug first.
  • Use short bursts of compressed air with the can upright, and hold fan blades still so they do not over-spin.
  • Do not use a household vacuum cleaner on internals: it can generate static.
  • Clean screens with a slightly damp microfibre cloth, never spraying liquid directly on them.
  • Clean every 3–6 months, more often in dusty places.

2. Preventive maintenance schedule

  • Weekly: check backups and install updates.
  • Monthly: check temperatures, free disk space and the antivirus.
  • Every 3–6 months: open the case, clean dust and check cables and fans.
  • Every 2–3 years (approximate): renew thermal paste and check battery health.
  • Use a surge protector or UPS against power surges and cuts, and keep the computer in a cool, ventilated place.

3. Corrective maintenance

Corrective maintenance fixes a fault after it appears: identify the problem, replace or repair the faulty part (fan, RAM, drive, PSU), reinstall or restore the software, then test. Always back up before repairs, and write down what you did in a service log. Records make future faults easier to trace.
